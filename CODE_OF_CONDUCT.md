# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (abridged)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Falcon Research Engine a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, or religion.

## Our Standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the research community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.
- **Never share live exchange credentials, account data, or trading capital
  details in issues, PRs, or examples.**

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the maintainers, who will review and take
appropriate action, including removal from the project space.
